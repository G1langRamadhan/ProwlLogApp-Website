8. World CATalog

World CATalog is an optional feature that lets you publish a cat from your private CATalog to a public catalog any ProwlLog user can browse, and lets you browse cats other users have published. Publishing is entirely your choice — nothing is published automatically.

  • What is published: the cat’s cover and thumbnail photo, name, breed, coat, the date you first recorded the cat, the display name on your profile, and an approximate country and location (rounded to about 0.1°, roughly 11 km). Your notes, address, precise coordinates, and feeding history are never published, and a published photo is re-encoded to remove embedded metadata such as GPS coordinates.
  • Limits: a free account may have up to 4 cats published at a time; ProwlLog Pro raises this to 10. If a Pro subscription lapses while more than 4 cats are published, nothing is taken down — you simply cannot publish again until you are back under the free limit. Browsing other users’ published cats beyond a limited free allowance requires ProwlLog Pro. A country must be determinable from the cat’s sighting location, or publishing will fail.
  • Your responsibility: you are solely responsible for a cat and photo you publish, including having the right to share it and ensuring it complies with Section 9 (Acceptable use and safety). Do not publish a photo that identifies another person, especially a child, without their (or their guardian’s) consent, and do not use the display name or published details to impersonate or mislead others about a cat’s ownership.
  • Removal: you can unpublish a cat at any time from Cat Detail or, if the private cat is no longer on your device, from the public viewer. We may also remove or decline to display published content, or suspend your ability to publish, at our discretion, if it violates these Terms, the in-app World CATalog rules, or applicable law — including in response to a report described below. We are not obliged to notify you before doing so, but will where reasonably practicable.

These controls are always available and are never limited by feature availability elsewhere in the app.

Published content is visible to any ProwlLog user, including people you do not know.
