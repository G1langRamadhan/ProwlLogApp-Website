Features

Everything you need to collect the cats you meet.

A playful, private way to remember every cat you cross paths with — no account required.

🗺️

Map of sightings

Every cat you log gets pinned right where you found it. Zoom out and nearby pins fold into one marker, so a busy street stays readable — tap it to flip through the cats you met there.

🐱

Your CATalog

A photo-led grid of every cat you've met, two per row. Tap any cat to open its page — photos, breed and coat, your notes, and exactly where you spotted it.

📷

Snap & pin

The in-app camera looks for a cat before it lets you shoot. Already took the photo? Pick it from your library and ProwlLog drops the pin where the shot was taken.

🍽️

Feeding log

Record when you fed a cat and what you gave them. Each cat keeps a Last Fed card and a full history grouped by day, so you always know who ate and when.

✨

Sticker Studio

Turn any cat photo into a clean cut-out sticker — full body, or just the head as a square or a circle — and send it anywhere you chat.

🌍

World CATalog · Full access with Pro

Publish a cat to a worldwide feed and browse cats spotted by ProwlLog users everywhere. Only the country travels with it — your exact location, notes and feeding history never do. Unpublish any time.

🔗

Share your collection

Send a private invite link and the friends you trust get read-only access to your cats, photos and sighting pins, synced through iCloud. Stop sharing whenever you like.

☁️

iCloud sync

Your cats, photos and feeding history mirror through your own private iCloud, so a new iPhone picks up right where you left off. Switch it off in Profile any time.

🔒

Private by design

No sign-up, no ProwlLog account. Your cats live on your device and in your private iCloud — nothing leaves it until you publish a cat to the World CATalog or send someone an invite link.

How it works

Three steps from sighting to collection.

1

Spot a cat

Out and about and you meet a cat? Open ProwlLog and get ready to add it.

2

Capture & pin

Snap a photo and pin the spot on the map, then give your new friend a name and details.

3

Grow your CATalog

Watch your map and collection fill up. Log their meals, make stickers, and share your favorites with friends or the whole world.
