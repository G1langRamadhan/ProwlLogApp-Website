Features

Everything you need to collect the cats you meet.

A playful, private way to remember every cat you cross paths with — no account required.

🗺️

Map of sightings

Every cat you log gets pinned right where you found it. Open the map and watch your whole neighborhood fill up with cats.

🐱

Your CATalog

A Pokédex-style grid of every cat you've met. Tap any card to see its photo, details, and where you spotted it.

📷

Snap & capture

Use the in-app camera to photograph the cat you just spotted, or add one from a quick form — it's in your collection in seconds.

🎖️

Badges, rings & shapes

Give each cat its own badge ring and shape. Dress up your favorites and make every card in your CATalog unique.

✨

Sticker Studio · Pro

Turn any cat photo into a clean sticker — full body or a head cut-out — and share it with friends anywhere.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no cloud. Every cat, photo, and map pin stays on your device — nothing is uploaded to us.

How it works

Three steps from sighting to collection.

1

Spot a cat

Out and about and you meet a cat? Open ProwlLog and get ready to add it.

2

Capture & pin

Snap a photo and pin the spot on the map, then give your new friend a name and details.

3

Grow your CATalog

Watch your collection and your map fill up with every cat you cross paths with.
