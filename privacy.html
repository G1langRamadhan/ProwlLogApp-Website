Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

ProwlLog (“we”, “our”, or “us”), operated by Gramadhan, respects your privacy. This policy explains how the ProwlLog app and this website handle your information. By using ProwlLog, you also agree to our Terms of Service.

1. Who we are

ProwlLog is an independent app made by Gramadhan. We are the party responsible for your information under this policy. For any privacy question or request, contact us at g1lan9r4madhan@gmail.com.

2. Information we access

Your cats, photos, map pins, badges, and collection are stored on your device, and ProwlLog does not require an account to use. ProwlLog accesses your camera to photograph cats you spot, your location (while you are using the app) to pin a sighting on the map, and your photo library (add-only) to save cat photos you choose. By default this information stays on your device and is not sent to us. The one exception is the optional Shared Collections feature (see Section 3), which uses Apple’s iCloud only when you choose to share a collection.

3. Shared Collections & iCloud (CloudKit)

Shared Collections is an optional ProwlLog Pro feature that lets you share your CATalog with other people and view collections that others have shared with you. It is built on Apple’s iCloud and CloudKit sharing (CKShare).

  • When it applies: This only happens if you are signed in to iCloud and choose to use sharing. If you never use sharing, your data stays on your device as described above.
  • What is stored in iCloud: To enable sharing, the collection you choose to share — which may include cat names, photos, map/location pins, and related details — is stored and synced through your iCloud account (CloudKit), which is operated by Apple.
  • What is shared with other people: Sharing happens only when you explicitly create and send an invite link. People you invite can view the collection you shared, including its cat photos and the location pins for those sightings.
  • Read-only access: Recipients have read-only access. They can view your shared collection but cannot edit, add to, or delete it.
  • Your control: You can stop sharing at any time, which revokes recipients’ access. Please only share collections with people you trust, and be mindful that shared sightings can reveal locations.

Data handled through iCloud/CloudKit is subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive a copy of your shared data on servers we operate.

4. Information we do not collect

ProwlLog does not collect your name, email, or contact information, device identifiers used for tracking, your contacts, calendar, or messages, or your browsing history. The location you attach to a cat sighting is saved on your device to place it on your map. We never receive it on our own servers; it is only made visible to others if you choose to share that collection through Shared Collections (see Section 3).

5. Permissions

The app asks for access to your camera (to photograph cats you spot), your location while using the app (to pin sightings on the map), and your photo library with add-only permission (to save cat photos you choose). Each permission is optional and requested only when needed. You can grant or revoke any of them at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.

6. No cross-app tracking

ProwlLog does not track you across other apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers (such as Apple’s IDFA), and does not display ads. We do not build advertising or marketing profiles about you.

7. Third-party services

ProwlLog Pro purchases are processed by Apple (StoreKit). We receive your subscription or purchase status from Apple to unlock Pro features, but never your payment details. The Shared Collections feature uses Apple’s iCloud/CloudKit to sync and share the collections you choose to share. If enabled in iOS, Apple may also share standard, anonymized crash and usage reports with us to help improve the app. Apple processes this information under Apple’s own Privacy Policy, which may involve processing in other countries.

8. Data storage & security

Your cats, photos, map pins, and settings are stored locally on your device and protected by iOS’s built-in security. ProwlLog does not require an account, and we do not operate our own servers that store your content. If you use Shared Collections, the data you share is stored in Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit) and protected under Apple’s security — we do not hold a copy on servers we operate.

9. Data retention

Because your content stays on your device, it is kept for as long as you keep ProwlLog installed. Uninstalling the app removes it. If you use Shared Collections, the data you shared also resides in your iCloud storage until you stop sharing or delete it. We do not keep copies.

10. Data deletion

You can delete individual cats and their photos at any time inside the app. Uninstalling ProwlLog removes all on-device app data — every cat, photo, and map pin — from your device. To remove shared data, stop sharing the collection (which revokes recipients’ access) and/or delete the shared items; you can also manage app data associated with iCloud in your device’s iCloud settings. You can also manage camera, location, and photo permissions anytime in iOS Settings.

11. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

If you are in the EEA or UK, we rely on these legal bases under the GDPR: performing our contract with you (providing the app and Pro features, including Shared Collections when you choose to use it) and our legitimate interest in improving the app (optional, anonymized diagnostics you can turn off). Because we do not collect personal data on our own servers, most processing — including any location you attach to a sighting — happens on your device, except for data you choose to share via Apple’s iCloud through Shared Collections.

12. Your rights

You can access your cats, photos, and map data directly in the app, and delete all app data by uninstalling ProwlLog. EU/UK users (GDPR) have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, and portability, and may lodge a complaint with their local data-protection authority. California users (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share personal information, and the rights to know and to delete apply through the in-app controls above.

13. Children’s privacy

ProwlLog is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app requires no account or personal data to function.

14. Website & cookies

This website may use essential cookies and privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics to keep the site running and understand overall traffic. It does not use advertising cookies or track you across other sites.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will indicate changes by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

16. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at g1lan9r4madhan@gmail.com.
