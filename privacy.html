Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

ProwlLog (“we”, “our”, or “us”), operated by Gramadhan, respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the ProwlLog app and this website process, why it is processed, where it is stored, and the choices available to you. It covers CATalog entries, sightings, feeding moments, iCloud sync, Shared Collections, purchases, and this website.

1. Who we are

ProwlLog is an independent app made by Gramadhan. For processing that we determine, Gramadhan is the responsible party under this policy. Apple separately processes information for the App Store, iCloud, CloudKit, StoreKit, and other Apple services under Apple’s own privacy terms. For any privacy question or request concerning ProwlLog, contact us at g1lan9r4madhan@gmail.com.

2. Information ProwlLog processes

ProwlLog may process the following information when you use the corresponding features:

  • CATalog content: cat names, photos, notes, profile details, collection information, badges, sticker selections, and other details you choose to add.
  • Sighting information: the cat associated with a sighting, the date and time, and a precise or approximate location or map pin when you choose to add one.
  • Feeding moments: the cat associated with a feeding entry, its date and time, and any other feeding details you choose to record.
  • Camera, photos, and location: content and location accessed through iOS permissions only when needed for the feature you request.
  • Purchase information: product, transaction, and entitlement status supplied by Apple so the app can determine whether Pro features are unlocked. We do not receive your full payment card or bank-account details.
  • Diagnostics: if you have chosen to share analytics or crash reports with developers through Apple, we may receive limited technical and diagnostic information to identify crashes and improve reliability.

ProwlLog does not require you to create a separate ProwlLog account. An Apple Account signed in to iCloud is required for iCloud-dependent features.

3. How we use information

Information is processed to provide the features you choose; save and organize your CATalog; display sightings on your map; maintain feeding histories; create stickers; sync supported data across your devices; share a collection with people you invite; verify and restore purchases; maintain security; diagnose faults; respond to support requests; and comply with applicable law.

4. On-device storage and iCloud sync

ProwlLog stores app data on your device. When iCloud sync is available and enabled, supported data is also stored in your private iCloud/CloudKit database and synchronized across devices signed in to your Apple Account. Supported synced data may include cats, photos and details, sightings and their map locations, and feeding moments. Syncing data to your private iCloud database does not by itself make it public or share it with another person.

Apple operates iCloud and CloudKit. Apple may process identifiers, device and network information, and the content stored through those services as described in Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not operate a separate server that receives or stores a copy of your CATalog content. iCloud availability, storage, security, and data recovery are controlled by Apple and your Apple Account settings.

5. Shared Collections

Shared Collections is an optional Pro feature built on Apple’s iCloud/CloudKit sharing service. It lets you share a CATalog with people you choose and view collections that others share with you.

  • When it applies: Sharing happens only when you deliberately create or accept a share or invitation. Ordinary private iCloud sync is separate from Shared Collections.
  • What may be shared: A shared collection may include cat names and details, photos, sightings, feeding moments, and precise or approximate map locations associated with it.
  • Who can access it: People you invite through the iCloud sharing flow can access the shared content. Apple processes information needed to provide the invitation and sharing service.
  • Read-only access: Recipients have read-only access. They can view your shared collection but cannot edit, add to, or delete it.
  • Your control: You can stop sharing to revoke future in-app access. A recipient may still retain information they previously copied or captured, such as a screenshot.

Share only with people you trust. Review a collection before sharing it because photos, notes, dates, feeding histories, and sighting locations may reveal sensitive information about you, another person, a home or business, or an animal’s routine.

6. Information we do not use for advertising

ProwlLog does not use advertising identifiers, serve third-party advertising, create advertising profiles, or track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your personal information. We also do not “share” personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as that term is defined by California privacy law. This does not refer to the user-directed Shared Collections feature described above.

7. Device permissions

Depending on the feature you use, ProwlLog may request camera access to take a photo, photo-library access to save photos or stickers you choose, and location access while using the app to place a sighting on the map. The system permission prompt shows the access requested. You can grant, limit, or revoke permissions in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security, although doing so may prevent the related feature from working.

8. When information is disclosed

Information may be disclosed to Apple to provide the App Store, StoreKit, iCloud, CloudKit, device permission, diagnostics, and related platform services; to recipients you deliberately invite through Shared Collections; when you choose to export or share a sticker, photo, or other content using the iOS share sheet; or when required to comply with applicable law or protect rights and safety. We do not give Apple your CATalog through a separate developer-operated server; the app uses Apple’s platform services directly.

9. Purchases

ProwlLog Pro subscriptions and the Nine Lives one-time purchase are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app receives transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock, manage, and restore access. Apple handles your payment method, billing data, and refunds under Apple’s terms and privacy policy.

10. Data security

On-device data benefits from the security features of your Apple device. Synced and shared data is protected through Apple’s iCloud and CloudKit security. We design ProwlLog to avoid operating a separate content server, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device passcode and Apple Account, keep your software current, and share sensitive content only with people you trust.

11. Retention and deletion

On-device content remains until you delete it in the app, erase the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Content synchronized through iCloud may remain in iCloud and on your other connected devices even after you uninstall ProwlLog from one device. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a Pro subscription.

You can delete supported cats, photos, sightings, and feeding entries within the app. When iCloud sync is enabled, deletions may propagate across your connected devices. To remove iCloud data, use the in-app deletion controls and, where necessary, manage ProwlLog data in your iCloud settings. Stop sharing a collection to revoke recipients’ future access. Copies already saved by a recipient are outside our control. Apple may retain limited records as required for security, fraud prevention, transactions, or law.

12. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, processing needed to provide features you request or manage a purchase is based on performance of our contract with you. Processing limited diagnostics and protecting the app may be based on our legitimate interests in reliability and security, balanced against your rights. We rely on consent where applicable law requires it, including permission choices controlled through iOS. We process information to comply with legal obligations where necessary.

13. Your choices and privacy rights

You can access, correct, and delete much of your information directly in ProwlLog; control camera, photo, location, analytics, and iCloud settings through your device; and stop sharing a collection in the app. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. These rights may be subject to legal exceptions. For data controlled by Apple, use Apple’s privacy tools or contact Apple. For a request concerning ProwlLog, email us using the address below.

14. International processing

Apple may process iCloud, App Store, purchase, and diagnostic information in countries other than where you live, subject to Apple’s safeguards and privacy policy. Because ProwlLog does not operate its own CATalog-content server, we do not independently transfer your CATalog to a developer-controlled server.

15. Children’s privacy

ProwlLog is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them through a developer-operated server. If you believe a child has provided information to us in a support request, contact us so we can address it.

16. Website and hosting

This is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. We do not place advertising cookies or analytics scripts on it. GitHub may process standard server-log information, such as your IP address, browser, device, requested page, and request time, to host and secure the site under GitHub’s privacy statement. If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond and retain the correspondence as reasonably needed for support and record-keeping.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will indicate changes by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If a change is material, we will provide additional notice where reasonably practicable or legally required.

18. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at g1lan9r4madhan@gmail.com.
